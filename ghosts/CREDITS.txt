New photo ghosts
================

These are the guides added for website slots that did not already have
one. The original twelve PNGs (qfront, front, driver, dash, console,
qrear, rear, tire, door, and the passenger flips of qfront, driver and
qrear) are untouched.

Each new file is a licensed photograph. The background was removed with
BiRefNet-general-lite when the subject could be separated from the
scene. A full-bleed interior that had no outdoor background kept a
feathered edge instead of an empty cut. The green is the same treatment
as the originals: luminance mapped into their green (R = 0.264 G,
B = 0.418 G), shaded fill, a brighter silhouette, and a short glow.

Regenerate with tools/photo_ghosts.py.

What still does not match a modern dealer photo of that exact slot:

- badge.png is the classic Toyota 2000GT emblem, not a modern trunk badge.
- keys.png is two key fobs. Service records are not in the frame.
- bedhitch.png is a spray-in bed liner in a truck bed. The hitch is not in the photo.
- sliding.png is a Honda Stepwgn with the sliding door shut. It frames the door, not an open aperture.
- folded.png is a 1988 Toyota Tarago with the seats down, not a current SUV.
- bedside.png is a three-quarter view of a 2021 F-150. The bed is visible; it is not a pure side elevation.
- sunroof.png is a wide cabin view that includes the glass roof, not a tight shot aimed only at the glass.
- screen.png, rearseat.png, legroom.png, sunroof.png and headliner.png keep a feathered full frame because those photos had no outdoor background for BiRefNet to remove.
