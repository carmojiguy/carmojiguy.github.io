Inspect

Trade-in, locate, general acquisition, or Canada Drives.

Press What’s the Story. The microphone turns on. Talk like you’re standing next to the vehicle with a buyer.

Say the year, make, model, trim or options, colour, and kilometres. Then walk the condition in detail: rust, dents, scratches, paintwork, glass, tires, interior, warning lights, noises, how it drives, accidents, service history, winter tires, extra keys — anything you’d mention in the driveway. Be specific. The more we know, the better.

If you have the ownership (registration), take a photo of it — that’s the easiest VIN. No ownership with you? Just read the 17-character VIN out loud while you tell the story.

Carfax, vAuto, OpenLane, eBlock — optional. Photograph the packet if you have it.

Best: take a picture of the vehicle ownership (registration). You can also photograph the VIN on the dash or the sticker on the driver’s door. We’ll read the number from the photo.

Damage

Skip this if the vehicle is clean. If something is damaged, tell us here — rust, a dent, a scratch, a cracked light, a panel that doesn’t open.

  1. Tap the part of the car that’s damaged using the same angles as the photos above (¾ front, driver side, rear, and so on).
  2. First tap turns the microphone on. Say what’s wrong, out loud — “rust on the front fender” or “dent in the passenger door.”
  3. Second tap takes the picture. Your words stick on the photo. Need a close-up of the same spot? Tap that panel again and shoot as many as you want.
