G&M studio backdrops
=====================

These plates are photographs, not drawn gradients.

Direction A (white, silver, warm) is the empty cyclorama
"Cyclorama hard light" by Sergej Majboroda, via Poly Haven, CC0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cyclorama_hard_light_-_Panorama_(Sergej_Majboroda_via_Poly_Haven).jpg
The panorama is projected to a low, close camera. The wall is lifted to a
clean light grey and the floor is held a little darker, with a faint
full-width seam so the floor reads as a large turntable. The shading is
the photograph's own.

Direction B (charcoal) is "Photo studio 01" by Sergej Majboroda, via Poly
Haven, CC0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photo_studio_01_–_Panorama_(Sergej_Majboroda_via_Poly_Haven).jpg
The lights and the room are left in the frame. The floor is darkened so it
reads as a polished surface under those lights.

The wall sign is the G&M wordmark (Liberation Sans Bold, the Arial-metric
face on this machine, red #E0243C ampersand and bar). On the white cove it
is small and centred at the top. On the photo studio it sits on the back
wall with a soft shadow.

Regenerate with tools/studio_photo.py. The proof composites seat the test
RAV4 and Camry at about 88% of the frame width, transfer the room's colour
onto the car, reflect a blurred piece of the plate in the glass, and lay a
fading floor reflection plus a contact shadow.

This machine has no GPU, so IC-Light and other diffusion relight models
were not run. The browser cannot run them either. web-studio.js places the
cutout on these plates at the same scale, with a simpler reflection. A
closer match needs a serverless harmonizer (IC-Light or a harmonization
net) that returns the relit car before the page draws it.
