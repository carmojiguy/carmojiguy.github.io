G&M studio backdrops
=====================

These plates are rendered for G&M Auto Sales & Service. They are not stock
photos and they replace the old generated gradient plates.

Each file is a photographic-style seamless light studio: softbox vignette,
circular turntable with concentric rings, and the approved G&M badge
(icons/gm-logo-square.png) centered on the wall.

Variants (4:3 is 3200×2400, 16:9 is 3200×1800):

- silver-4x3.jpg / silver-16x9.jpg — light grey wall, silver turntable
- white-4x3.jpg / white-16x9.jpg — bright white studio
- warm-4x3.jpg / warm-16x9.jpg — warm grey, thin G&M red turntable edge
- charcoal-4x3.jpg / charcoal-16x9.jpg — dark charcoal studio, lit turntable

Regenerate with tools/render_gm_studio.py (Pillow + NumPy). The badge artwork
is the approved square logo already in this repo.

Cars are cut out with BiRefNet-general-lite for the proof contact sheet, and
in the app with @imgly/background-removal (ISNet, medium) running in the
browser. If that model fails to load, the original photo is kept and no plate
is pasted behind it. Backgrounds are applied to full exterior views only.
