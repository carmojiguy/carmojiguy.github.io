G&M studio backdrops
=====================

These plates are rendered for G&M Auto Sales & Service. They are not stock
photos and they replace the old generated gradient plates.

Each file is a seamless studio cove: softboxes, a vignette, and a plain
turntable. The disc is a flat satin surface (dark grey on the light-grey,
warm, and charcoal studios; polished light grey on the white studio). It has
a thin bevelled metal edge with one soft specular highlight on the near-left
arc, and a gentle floor gradient. There are no concentric rings.

The wall sign is the G&M wordmark itself (heavy grotesque, red #E0243C
ampersand and bar, tracked "AUTO SALES & SERVICE"). There is no black badge
box. Dark letters on the light studios, white letters on charcoal.

The ellipse is built for a low dealer camera (about bumper height): flatter
than a turntable photographed from above the roof. A much higher camera
would need a rounder disc than these plates provide.

Variants (4:3 is 3200×2400, 16:9 is 3200×1800):

- silver-4x3.jpg / silver-16x9.jpg — light grey wall, dark satin turntable
- white-4x3.jpg / white-16x9.jpg — bright white studio, polished light-grey disc
- warm-4x3.jpg / warm-16x9.jpg — warm grey wall, dark satin turntable
- charcoal-4x3.jpg / charcoal-16x9.jpg — dark charcoal studio, satin turntable

Regenerate with tools/render_gm_studio.py (Pillow + NumPy). The wordmark is
set in Liberation Sans Bold, the Arial-metric face available here, stacked
to a heavy weight. Proof composites are tools/studio_proof.py using
BiRefNet-general-lite, with edge defringe, tinted glass, per-tire contact
shadows, a soft body shadow, a faint cool rim light, and a short floor
reflection. The lowest tire rubber is seated on the near half of the disc
and the car is centred on it.

In the app, @imgly/background-removal ISNet large runs in the browser and
falls back to the medium model. If that fails to load, the original photo
is kept and no plate is pasted behind it. Backgrounds stay on full exterior
views only.
